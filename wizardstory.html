Tale of the Great Ancient Spellcaster

Long long long ago, in a world where wizards were constantly in war, an unknown spellcaster rose to the top of the conflict. From ashes to dust, an ordinary boy found his name in a town called Kojavalopinaakokawa'Astriolmonakipa. He was abused by his family and so, refuged in the town of Kojavalopinaakokawa'Astriolomonakipa. Arriving to the village, he was taken in by an old woman and got taken care of. Years later, when the old woman fell ill, she gave him her last gift: a book of spells. He slowly started to learn and seemed to take interest in it, he practiced and practiced until he mastered a simple spell. To understand more about it, he went to the local library in the town. He found a book laying on the ground and so, he picked it up. He scrolled past pages and pages of the book until he landed on one specific type of magic: Black Magic.
